How to play iMBSE

01

Type what you mean

Commands are short: GO NORTH (or just N), TAKE LANTERN, SHOW TABLET TO GOLEM, PRY BLOB WITH RULE, THROW DUCK AT BUG. LOOK re-reads a room, EXAMINE studies a thing, HELP lists the verbs.

07

Paste a whole column

The box takes more than one command at a time. Paste a column of them — or separate them with ; or a full stop — and they play out one at a time so you can watch. ESC, or the Stop button, halts a batch part-way. Handy with the walkthrough, and with any route you have written down.

02

The lantern burns context

It spends tokens only while it is lit — 330 of them. Light it going into the dark, put it out coming back into the light. The Token Fountain will refill it, for a price you may not want to pay.

03

Seven halls, seven artifacts

One keystone artifact from each functional area, and thirteen lesser treasures besides — twenty in all. They score when found and score properly when socketed in the Digital Thread Vault. Read the placard in every hall: each one names the face it beats.

04

You can carry seven things

That is a puzzle, not an annoyance. The balloon basket carries cargo up for you and the Atrium is the natural place to stage everything else.

05

Talk to everybody

Five people live here who are not trying to kill you. PLAY Tom's games for story points, ANSWER Alexander's riddles for true hints, ask Katie for a VISION, and ask Saint Mark about Dassault — it is the most useful thing anybody says.

06

Buy the blasting cap

The charge in the endgame is inert and the shop does not exist after the baseline freezes. Everything else at the Company Store is optional. That one is not.
