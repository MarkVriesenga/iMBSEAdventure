# iMBSE — the play-through commands

Every command of the verified route, in order: **565 of them across 18 stages**, ending on **741 of 750** with twenty treasures banked, all 124 rooms entered and no deaths.

**Paste a block, don't type it.** The command box takes a column of commands and plays them out one at a time, so you can watch the map and the score move. `ESC`, or the Stop button, halts a batch part-way.

The bugs are weather, and they're not in here. A bug interrupt will derail a pasted block — that's what the rubber duck is for (`THROW DUCK AT BUG`, then take it back). The static analyser from the Company Store buys 100 turns of quiet.

Generated by `node tools/play.js --md commands.md` from `docs/verify/route-commands.js`. Don't hand-edit it: change the route, rebuild, and run the tests. Prose, traps and the reasoning behind the route are in [docs/WALKTHROUGH.md](docs/WALKTHROUGH.md).
